Establish Link Phase
In this phase, you can establish a link starting from an active satellite. Establishing links leads to fame from several sources, such as completing Mission cards and the number of linked regions, so we recommend establishing links with 8-10 or more areas in order to win.
Main Action
Action Selection Bonus

Each player takes one of their own cubes and, all at the same time, places it on a linkable area or outside the game board. Where the cube is placed means:

a.Placed on an area: establish a link
b.Placed outside the game board: abandon the Establish Link action

Each player's linkable areas are the areas that satisfy all of the following conditions.

i.An area where one of your satellite pieces is located (Project card effects may expand this range)
ii.An area where neither your launch base piece nor your cube has been placed
iii.An area whose link cost you can pay

To establish a link, you must pay two things as the link cost: the area cost and the pioneer cost.

a.Area cost: Land areas cost 5 , Sea areas cost 2 .
b.Pioneer cost: You must pay 3 to each player who already has a cube placed on that area.

The link cost cannot become less than 0 (a negative value) through card abilities or other effects (it never goes below 0).

Your satellite in the area where you placed a cube is regarded as having established the link. However, if several of your satellites can link the same area, you may freely choose which satellite established the link.

The Action Selection Bonus is performed, after everyone has established links at the same time, only by the players who chose the “IV Establish Link” action. Each such player, as before, places a cube on a linkable area or outside the game board, all at the same time. For the Action Selection Bonus, condition (i) for linkable areas is expanded, while conditions (ii) and (iii) and the link cost are the same as before.

i.An area where one of your satellite pieces is located, and the adjacent areas directly ahead of or behind the satellite
Example 1: In the Establish Link action, Teresa (blue) placed a cube on the area in the right figure. Teresa has a satellite “HAMMER” on that area and has no cube placed there, so Teresa can link it. The area is a Land area and Jessie (green)'s cube is already placed there, so Teresa paid 5 to the supply as the area cost and 3 to Jessie as the pioneer cost. Being the 2nd to place a cube in this area, Teresa placed the cube in the upper-right space.
Example 2: In the Establish Link action, Luna (red) and Lily (yellow) placed cubes on the red-framed area. Luna has no satellite on the red-framed area, but because of the effect of “Continental Antenna (P052)” deployed to Luna’s satellite “RETRO,” the red-framed area, a Land area adjacent to “RETRO,” is also linkable for Luna. Luna and Lily paid 5 , the link cost of a Mountain area, to the supply as the link cost. Because Luna and Lily were the first to place cubes on this area, they placed their cubes stacked on the upper-left cube space, and each draw 1 Project card as the area bonus.
Example 3: In the Establish Link action, Jessie (green) placed a cube outside the game board and abandoned the link. This is because Jessie had only 2 and wanted to save .
Example 4: In the extra action of the Action Selection Bonus, Teresa (blue) placed a cube on the red-framed area. This is because Teresa has a satellite HAMMER at the lower left of that area and HAMMER is heading toward the upper right, so the red-framed area, the area ahead in its heading, is linkable. This area was the area that Jessie (green) linked as the Main Action of the Establish Link Phase. Although it is the same Establish Link Phase, the Main Action and the bonus action are separate steps, so Teresa must pay 3 to Jessie as the pioneer cost.
