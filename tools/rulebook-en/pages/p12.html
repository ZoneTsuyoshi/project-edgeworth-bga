This game is played in units called “rounds.” Rounds repeat until an end condition is met. Each round consists of 3 phases: the Planning Phase, the Action Phase, and the Orbit Phase. The Action Phase is divided into 5 phases, and only the phases chosen by at least one player take place.

The Planning Phase is the phase for deciding the actions that will be carried out this round. When choosing an Action Card, we recommend keeping in mind that, as a rule, you cannot choose the same action in consecutive rounds (except I Budget), and that you should watch the other players so that several of the actions you want to take are carried out. Also, a Deploy Phase is performed once during setup, but the “II Deploy” Action Card has not been used, so you can choose “II Deploy” in the first round.

Only the phases chosen by at least one player in the Planning Phase become the actions carried out this round. The chosen actions are carried out in order of their Roman numerals, from the smallest (I to V). In each phase, players who chose that action receive the effect of the “Action Selection Bonus.”
