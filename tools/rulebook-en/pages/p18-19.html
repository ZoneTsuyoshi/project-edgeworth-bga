Orbit Phase
In the Orbit Phase, all active satellites are subject to “orbit.” Orbit is carried out in the order “Satellite Movement,” “Fuel Consumption,” “Near Miss Check,” and “Landing / Splashdown Check.” Also, if the game end condition is met at the end of the Orbit Phase, the game ends immediately.
Satellite Movement

Move each satellite piece 1 area in the direction its satellite is heading, all at the same time. If a satellite would move off the game board from an area at the top, bottom, left, or right edge, determine the area it moves to using the following boundary conditions.

a.Off the left or right edge: Treat the left and right edges of the game board as connected, move the satellite piece to the opposite side, and continue in the same heading.
b.Off the top or bottom edge: Flip the satellite piece’s heading up or down to perform a “bounce,” then move the satellite piece 1 area after the bounce. A “bounce” is distinct from a “turn,” so effects triggered by a “turn” are not triggered by a bounce.

On very rare occasions, several players may each have a satellite with a card effect that moves it additional areas, and whether each one moves depends on the other player’s choice. In that case, process them in order, starting with the player closest to the base player in clockwise order.

Fuel Consumption
Each satellite uses 1 fuel. Fuel consumption is shown by moving the fuel marker on that satellite’s satellite card and on its deployed Project cards up by one fuel slot.
Example 4: In Lily (yellow)’s satellite “STEALTH,” Lily moves the fuel marker from the top fuel slot on the satellite card to the bottom fuel slot of the first Project card deployed.
Near Miss Check
If, after all satellite pieces have moved, there are several satellites in the same area, those satellites are considered to have had a “near miss.” Each satellite that had a near miss uses 2 fuel.
Example 5: After the satellite pieces move, Jessie (green)’s satellite “SCALLOP” and Teresa (blue)’s satellite “HAMMER” are in the same area. Therefore, each of the 2 satellites uses 2 fuel.
Landing / Splashdown Check
If the fuel marker moves off the top fuel slot of the Project card placed highest on that satellite, the satellite will land / splash down. A satellite that has landed / splashed down is removed from the game board and placed on its satellite card. Also, if the area where it splashed down was a Sea area, the player who owns that satellite gains 10 .
Example 6: Teresa (blue)’s satellite “CONCORDE” ran out of fuel because of “Fuel Consumption.” That is, its fuel marker moved off the top fuel slot of the top Project card (1). Teresa’s satellite “CONCORDE” and Jessie (green)’s satellite “HAMMER” are in the same area, so they are considered to have had a near miss, and Jessie’s satellite “HAMMER” uses 2 additional fuel (2). Teresa’s satellite “CONCORDE” is already out of fuel, but since this is before the Landing / Splashdown Check, the 2 satellites are still considered to have had a near miss. Therefore, Jessie’s satellite “HAMMER” has used a total of 3 fuel during this Orbit Phase. Teresa’s satellite “CONCORDE” ran out of fuel in a Sea area, so it is considered to have splashed down: Teresa gains 10 and returns the satellite piece to its satellite card (3).
