VIII: Reward cards (link-related)
As a Reward card, “Sea Hegemony” was used in the game. “Sea Hegemony” compares the number of linked Sea areas. Teresa had linked 14 Sea areas, Jessie 5, Luna 8, and Lily 8 as well. Since Teresa linked the most Sea areas, Teresa gains 18, the fame for “1st” on “Sea Hegemony.” Luna and Lily tied for the 2nd most with 8 Sea areas, so they add the fame for “2nd” and “3rd,” divide by 2 (rounding down), and each gains 9. Jessie had the fewest, placing 4th, and gains no fame.
