Launch / Turn Phase
In this phase, you can launch and turn satellites. You cannot establish links until you launch a satellite, and your options are limited, so we recommend launching your first satellite within the first few rounds.
Main Action

Each player chooses 1 standby satellite piece and, all at the same time, places it on the game board or on an active satellite card. This means:

a.Placed on the game board: the “Launch” action
b.Placed on an active satellite card: the “Turn” action

Launch

A player who wants to launch a satellite places the satellite piece they want to launch on the area containing their launch base, facing the direction they want to launch it. A player who launches a satellite piece pays equal to the launch cost printed on the satellite card. To launch, both of the following 2 conditions must be met:

1.It is on standby
2.At least 1 Project card has been deployed to it

Turn

A player who wants to turn chooses any standby satellite piece and places it on the satellite card of the active satellite they want to turn, facing the direction they want to turn it. The satellite being turned turns in the chosen direction.

Action Selection Bonus

The Action Selection Bonus is carried out as an additional bonus action after everyone’s Launch / Turn action. If multiple players perform the additional action, each of those players takes 1 of their standby satellite pieces and, all at the same time, places it on a satellite card in any direction.

a.Placed on an active satellite card: the “Turn” bonus action
b.Placed on a standby satellite card: the “Budget increase” bonus action

Budget increase

A player who chose Budget increase gets +1 .

Example 1: Luna (red) took the satellite piece of the standby satellite “RETRO.” When everyone placed their satellite pieces at the same time, Luna placed the “RETRO” satellite piece on the area containing their launch base, facing the upper right. Luna paid 20 as the launch cost of “RETRO,” and gained +2 as the On launch effect of “RETRO.” This satellite will move toward the upper right in the Orbit Phase.
Example 2: Lily (yellow) took the “RETRO” satellite piece of a standby satellite. When everyone placed their satellite pieces at the same time, Lily placed the “RETRO” satellite piece on their own “HAMMER” satellite card, facing the lower left. Lily turned the “HAMMER” satellite piece on the game board to face the lower left. This satellite will move toward the lower left in the Orbit Phase.
Example 3: Jessie (green) and Luna (red) had both chosen the “III Launch / Turn” action in the Planning Phase. Because multiple players receive the Action Selection Bonus of “III Launch / Turn,” Jessie and Luna must each take a standby satellite and, at the same time, choose either the “Turn” action or the “Budget increase” action.
Example 4: With the additional action of the Action Selection Bonus, Luna (red) took the satellite piece of the standby satellite “STEALTH.” When the satellite pieces were placed at the same time for the additional action, Luna placed the “STEALTH” satellite piece on the card of their own standby satellite “SCALLOP.” This means Luna chose Budget increase, so Luna gets +1 .
Proposal Phase
In this phase, you can replenish your Project cards. We recommend choosing it especially when a Reward card calls for a specific type of Project card.
Main Action
Draw 2 Project cards, add 1 to your hand, and discard the other 1.
Action Selection Bonus
Replace the Main Action above with the following. Draw 5 Project cards, add 2 to your hand, and discard the other 3.
