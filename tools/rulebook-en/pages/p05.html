Each area has 4 places for cubes. Starting with the first player to establish a link there, cubes are placed in order in a Z pattern. If players establish links at the same time, their cubes are stacked, and each player can get the higher area bonus. When establishing a link with an area where cubes are stacked, place the new cube skipping as many places as there are cubes already.
Example: In the area shown at right, Teresa (blue) and Lily (yellow) have placed cubes in the upper left (1st place). If Jessie (green) places a cube in this area, it goes in the lower left, the place of the 3rd player, not the upper right, the place of the 2nd player. As a result, Jessie cannot get the area bonus of the Deep Sea area.
Track Board
The track board has the Budget track on its front side and the Fame track on its back side. We recommend using the front side during the game and the back side when calculating fame at the end of the game.
Action Cards
You are given a set of 5 Action Cards at the start of the game. By choosing one in the Planning Phase of each round, you make the corresponding action available. Because you can also carry out actions chosen by other players, the best Action Card to choose is not always the action you most want to do; at times you will also need to read which actions the other players are likely to choose.
