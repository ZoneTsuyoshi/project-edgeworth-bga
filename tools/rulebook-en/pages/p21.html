Deciding the Winner
Add up the points from each of the following elements. The player with the highest total wins. If players are tied, they share the victory.
Budget: 1 per point of Budget
Maya: 1 per 10
Linked areas: Land area 2, Sea area 1
Linked regions (): Fame according to the number of different regions in which you have a cube, as follows.
Mission cards: Fame according to mission completion
Active satellites: Fame printed on the satellite card
Project cards:
Cards deployed on active satellites: total fame printed on the cards
Cards deployed on standby satellites: half of the total fame printed on the cards (rounded down)
all on one satellite: 5 per satellite
Reward cards: For each Reward card, players are compared and receive fame according to their rank
End condition: A player who met an end condition gets 3

We recommend flipping over the Budget track and using the Fame track to calculate fame. To avoid losing track of what your Budget was, we recommend resolving the fame gained from Budget first. If there is a Reward card related to Budget (“T013: Star Vein Hegemony”), we recommend resolving it first at this point as well.

The Fame track corresponds to the size of the cubes, so we recommend using spare cubes or fuel markers. In the rare case that you have met both end conditions and have neither cubes nor fuel markers left over, substitute something else, such as disc pieces.

When your fame exceeds 100, we recommend placing your spare cubes or the like on “+100” so that you can keep track.
