The following rules apply to the game.
i.At any time, you may discard 1 Project card to gain 3 .
ii.Up to 4 Project cards can be deployed to one satellite.
iii.The “special link cost” mentioned in card effects means the “link cost” plus a “special cost” of 5 that you must pay. Unless otherwise stated, a special link can be established only with the area directly beneath that satellite. However, if that satellite has an effect that expands its linkable areas, that effect is applied.
Example: Teresa (blue)’s satellite HAMMER has “Forward Wide-Area Transmitter (P056)” deployed to it. As Teresa’s Main Action of “IV Establish Link,” Teresa established a link with the red-framed area directly beneath HAMMER (1). Forward Wide-Area Transmitter’s effect is triggered when its satellite establishes a link, so Teresa pays the special link cost for the effect. By paying it, Teresa can establish a link with the green-framed area, the adjacent area ahead of HAMMER (2). In the green-framed area, Jessie (green) had established a link as Jessie’s Main Action of “IV Establish Link.” Teresa must therefore pay, as the special link cost, the area cost of 5 and the special cost of 5 to the supply, and the pioneer cost of 3 to Jessie.
When this satellite establishes a link, you may additionally pay the special link cost. If you do, additionally establish a link with the adjacent area ahead of this satellite’s heading.
Special link timings may occasionally overlap among multiple players. If the players’ choices change each other’s actions, effects are resolved in order, clockwise from the base player. In this case, if multiple players establish a link with the same area, the timings are considered simultaneous.
End Conditions
The game ends if any of the following is met at the end of a round.
i.Any player has all 5 of their satellites active.
ii.Any player has established 16 links. In other words, the cubes for establishing links have run out.
