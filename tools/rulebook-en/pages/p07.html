Satellite Card
Satellite cards are handed out as a set of 5 at the start of the game. Each satellite card corresponds to one satellite piece and, like Project cards, gives that satellite, or yourself, a variety of effects. For your first game, we recommend using the “MOON” plan shared by all players. After you have played several times, we recommend using the side that is not “MOON” to enjoy each player's unique effects.
