Region Board
Areas
Region boards have a front side and a back side. The side made up only of Sea and Land areas is the front side, and the side that also includes other areas is the back side. Before the game starts, decide whether to use the front side or the back side.
Area. One region consists of 9 areas in a 3x3 layout.
Region icon. The front and back have the same region icon.
Region border. The boundary with another region.

The front side of a region board has 2 types of areas, Sea and Land; the back side has 7 types. Each area has an “area cost,” which is required to establish a link with that area, and “area fame,” which is earned at the end of the game. Areas that appear only on the back side of a region board also have an “area bonus,” which is earned according to the order in which links are established.
