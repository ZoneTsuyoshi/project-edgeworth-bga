Once you are comfortable with the game, you can also add option rules for more fun.
Smaller Game Board
Normally, region boards are laid out in a 2x4 arrangement, but in a game with 2-3 players you can play on a smaller game board. In a 2-player game, you can play with a 2x2 or 2x3 board layout; in a 3-player game, with a 2x3 board layout. Doing so will probably make the interaction between players more intense.
Project Card Draft
If you are experienced with board games, we recommend choosing your first 5 Project cards by drafting. Drafting is a method in which everyone secretly chooses 1 card from the 5 cards dealt to them, passes the remaining cards to the player on their left, then chooses 1 card from the 4 cards passed to them and passes the rest to the player on their left, and repeats this. In this way, each player keeps 1 card at a time and ends up with 5 cards in hand in total. It will make combos easier to build and let you enjoy playing with the other players' cards in mind.
