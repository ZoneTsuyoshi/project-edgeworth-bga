Decide which side of the region boards to use, shuffle them, and arrange them in a 2x4 layout as shown in the figure at right. For your first game, we recommend using the front side and the same arrangement as the figure.
Place the Budget track next to the region boards.
Shuffle the Project cards well and place them where everyone can reach them.
Gather the Maya chips and place them where everyone can reach them.
Place 2 Reward cards face up. For your first game, we recommend using 1 card from the Reward cards and 1 card from the Reward cards. Put the remaining Reward cards back in the box, as they will not be used.
The player who most recently used a smartphone is the “base player.”
Each player chooses a player color they like from blue, green, red, and yellow.
Give each player the 5 satellite cards of their color. For your first game, use the satellite cards of the MOON plan. The MOON plan is the same set of satellite cards for all players. Once you are used to the game, flipping the satellite cards over gives each player a different set of satellite cards, so you can enjoy a new way to play.

Starting with the base player and going clockwise, each player places their launch base on a Land area that no one has placed one on yet. When playing on the back side of the region boards, you cannot gain an area bonus by placing a launch base.
Give each player a set of 5 Action Cards.
Each player places their disc on the 15 space of the Budget track.
Deal 2 Mission cards to each player. Put the remaining Mission cards back in the box, as they will not be used.
