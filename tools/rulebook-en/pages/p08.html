Mission Card
Each player is dealt 2 Mission cards at the start of the game. Completing both Missions in full is difficult, so we recommend deciding by mid-game which one to focus on. There is also a trade-off between completing Missions and the fame you earn in the end-of-game scoring (explained later) for the number of regions you have linked, so we recommend deciding by mid-game which to prioritize.
