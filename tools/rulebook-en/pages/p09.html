Reward Card
Two Reward cards are chosen for the whole game at the start of the game. At the end of the game, players earn fame in order of their value for the comparison shown on the Reward card: the higher the value, the more fame. Because the fame from Reward cards is large, we recommend aiming for a high rank on at least one of the two rewards.

Reward comparison
Fame earned at the end of the game by the player of that rank. If more than one player holds that rank, add up the fame for as many ranks as there are such players, divide the total by the number of those players (rounding down), and each player of that rank earns that amount. Also, a player whose comparison value for the reward is 0 earns no fame.

Example 2: For the Reward card “Sea Hegemony” above, the numbers of linked Sea areas were 9 for Teresa, 5 for Jessie, and 0 for Lily and Luna. Because the reward comparison value of Lily and Luna is 0, they cannot earn any fame.
