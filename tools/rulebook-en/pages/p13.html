Budget Phase
In this phase, you can gain funds (, called “Maya”).
Main Action
Each player gains equal to their Budget .
Action Selection Bonus
Gain an additional 6 .
Example: Teresa chose the “I Budget” action. If the Budget values of Teresa, Jessie, Luna, and Lily are 17, 18, 20, and 18, they gain 23=17+6, 18, 20, and 18 , respectively.
Deploy Phase
In this phase, you can deploy Project cards to satellite cards to strengthen your satellites.
Main Action
Choose 1 Project card from your hand, pay the deployment cost printed on the Project card, and deploy it to a satellite of your choice. However, when deploying a card to an active satellite, you must pay an additional deployment cost of 5 . Also, each satellite can have at most 4 Project cards deployed to it.
Action Selection Bonus
Deploy 1 additional Project card, or draw 1 Project card.
Example: Jessie wants to deploy “Solar Panel,” which has a deployment cost of 6, to their active satellite “RETRO.” Jessie pays 6+5=11 and places “Solar Panel” on top of the topmost Project card stacked on that satellite card.
